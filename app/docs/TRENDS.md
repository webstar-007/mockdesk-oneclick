# SSC CGL Tier 2 (Paper I) — trend analysis used to shape the mocks

Sources: Time4Education analysis of CGL 2024 Tier-2 (18 & 20 Jan 2025), PW / SATHEE topic-weightage
pages, Oliveboard and Drishti pattern pages (2025-26). Papers themselves were NOT copied; only the
topic distribution, style and difficulty were used.

Pattern: 150 Q. Maths 30, Reasoning 30, English 45, GA 25, Computer 20. +3 per correct, −1 per wrong.

## Observed difficulty (2024 Tier 2)
Maths easy-moderate (≈15 easy, 14 moderate, 1 hard) but calculation-heavy; Reasoning easy-moderate;
English moderate; GA (static GK) DIFFICULT; General Science moderate; Computer easy-moderate.
Our "Moderate" mocks (1-2) match this; our "Hard" mocks (3-5) are deliberately above it
(multi-step, trap options, closer distractors, harder GK) for over-preparation.

## Maths — questions per 30 (two shifts, averaged)
Ratio & proportion 2-4 | Percentages 2 | Profit & loss/discount 2 | Simple & compound interest 1-2 |
Time & work 1 | Time-speed-distance (trains, boats) 1 | Averages 1 | Mixtures & alligation 1 |
Number system (remainders, HCF/LCM, divisibility) 0-2 | Geometry (triangles, circles, centres,
tangents, polygons) 2-3 | Mensuration (2D & 3D, cones/cylinders/spheres/frustum) 4 |
Algebra (identities, x+1/x, cubic identities, equations) 2-4 | Indices & surds 1-2 |
Trigonometry (identities, heights & distances, values) 3 | Probability 1 | Statistics / Data
Interpretation (mean/median/mode, tables, pie/bar given as table) 2-3 | Simplification/speed maths 1.
Style: values chosen so that the 4 options are close; traps = unit slips, successive vs sum
percentages, CI vs SI, "ratio of areas vs sides", rounding.

## Reasoning — questions per 30
Verbal/logical: coding-decoding 2, blood relations 2, symbols & notations / math operations 2,
data sufficiency 2, syllogism 1-2, logical order of words / ranking 2.
Critical reasoning: inferences 1, courses of action 1, cause & effect 1, strong/weak arguments 1,
statement–conclusion 1. Analytical puzzles: linear arrangement 1, circular arrangement 1,
distribution/scheduling 1, comparison 1, ranking/order 1. Miscellaneous: letter series, number
series 2, letter/number/word analogy 3, odd one out (letters/numbers/words) 3, number arrangement,
meaningful words / dictionary order 1, direction & distance 1, calendar or clock 1, Venn 1.
NO figure/image based questions are possible — use text-based variants only.

## English — questions per 45
Reading comprehension 12 (2-3 passages, 200-300 words, inference/vocab-in-context/tone/main idea) |
Cloze test 4-5 (one passage with blanks) | Fill in the blanks 3-4 | Para jumble (sentence & part
based) 4-6 | Direct/indirect speech 3 | Active/passive voice 3 | Error identification 2-3 |
Antonyms 3 | Synonyms 1-2 | One-word substitution 2-5 | Idioms & phrases 2-3 | Spelling (misspelt/
correct word) 2 | Homophones 1-2 | Word meaning/usage or sentence improvement 1-2.
Hard mocks: denser vocabulary (GRE-lite but SSC style), subtler grammar traps (subjunctive,
parallelism, article/preposition collocations, pronoun-antecedent), longer inference passages.

## General Awareness — per 25
Static GK ≈ 17-19 (Geography 3-6, Polity 2-5, Economy 3-5, History 3-4, Sports/Books/Misc 1-2) and
Science 6-8 (Chemistry 2-4, Biology 2-3, Physics 1-2). Current affairs are excluded (cannot be kept
accurate for a fixed bank).

## Computer — per 20
Software (MS Word/Excel/PowerPoint, shortcuts, formulas, OS) 7-8 | Computer basics (hardware,
memory, number systems, generations, I/O devices) 4-7 | Networking & cyber security 4-6 |
Internet & email 2.
