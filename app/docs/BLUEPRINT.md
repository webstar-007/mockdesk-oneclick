# SSC CGL Tier 2 (Paper I) — Pattern, Trend Analysis, Mock Blueprint

## 1. Pattern used (central config: `src/config/exam.ts`)

| Section | Subject | Qs | Marks/Q | Negative | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | Mathematical Abilities | 30 | 3 | −1 | 30 min (sectional) |
| I | Reasoning & General Intelligence | 30 | 3 | −1 | 30 min (sectional) |
| II | English Language & Comprehension | 45 | 3 | −1 | 40 min (sectional) |
| II | General Awareness | 25 | 3 | −1 | 20 min (sectional) |

150 questions, 135 minutes. Computer Knowledge is a qualifying paper (pass mark 27 of 60) and is not added to the total, so the scored total is 130 questions = 390 marks. The Data Entry Speed Test (Session II) is a separate typing test and is not simulated.

Sources: SSC Tier 2 pattern summaries (Drishti SSC, PW, Time4Education analysis of CGL 2024 Tier II held 18/20 Jan 2025).
Sectional timing is a 2026 change; Section groupings (I: 60 min, II: 60 min) were used before. The config supports both.

## 2. Trend analysis (what recent papers actually tested)

Detailed topic counts are published for CGL 2024 Tier II (Jan 2025, two days). Earlier cycles (Tier II of CGL 2022 and 2023) are covered only qualitatively.

**Maths (30; section id "maths"):** Mensuration 4, Trigonometry 3, Ratio 2–4, Geometry 2–3, Percentage 2, Profit & Loss 2, Algebra 2–3, Statistics 2, SI/CI 1–2, Time-Work 1, Speed-Distance 1–2, Averages 1, Mixture 1, Number system/Surds 1–2. Difficulty: easy–moderate with 1 hard; calculation-heavy rather than concept-heavy. Typical styles: frustum/cone/sphere melting-recasting, sinθ/cosθ identities, x+1/x algebra, incentre/circumcentre angle chasing, successive discount, boat & stream, pipes.

**Reasoning (30):** verbal/logical 10–11 (blood relations, coding-decoding, data sufficiency, syllogism, logical order, symbols & operations), non-verbal 6–7, misc 7 (series, odd one out, analogy, meaningful words), analytical puzzles 3 (seating/arrangement), critical reasoning 3 (assumption, strengthen/weaken, course of action). Easy–moderate; time-saving.

**English (45):** vocabulary 12–15 (synonyms, antonyms, idioms, one-word substitution, spelling), grammar ~11 (error spotting, sentence improvement, fill in blanks, voice, narration), para/sentence formation 3–6 (jumbles), comprehension ~16 (1 cloze + 3 RC; long passages are the main source of difficulty).

**General Awareness (25):** GK ~17 (Polity 2–5, Economy 3–5, Geography 3–6, History 3–4, Sports 1–2, Books/awards/static 1–2) + Science ~8 (Chemistry 2–4, Biology 2–3, Physics 1–2). GK has been the hardest segment. NOTE: our mocks use STATIC awareness only (no dated current-affairs), so that every answer remains verifiable.

**Computer (20):** MS Office/software 7–8, fundamentals 4–7, networking & cyber-security 4–6, internet & email 2. Basic-to-moderate; shortcut keys, Excel functions, memory units, protocols.

## 5. Quality rules (every question)
- Original wording and numbers. Never copy past-paper questions. Reflect the same concept/style/difficulty only.
- Exactly one defensible correct answer. No "all of the above/none of these" unless unavoidable and verified.
- Distractors must be plausible and built from genuine common errors.
- Correct option position must be roughly uniform (each of A–D 20–30% per file).
- Quant: every numeric answer MUST be computed and checked by a Python script you run (print a verification table). Use clean integer/simple-fraction answers.
- Use plain Unicode for maths: x², x³, √, π, °, ≤, ≥, ×, ÷, − and "a/b" for fractions. NO LaTeX, no `$`, no HTML.
- GA/Computer: only include facts you are certain are correct and stable (no events after 2023, no disputed claims, no record-holders that change). If unsure, choose a different fact.
- English: no ambiguity in grammar items (a single clear rule decides). Passages are original (written by you), not copied.
